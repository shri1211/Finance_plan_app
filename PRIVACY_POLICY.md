# Privacy Policy for Finance Tracker

**Last Updated:** May 13, 2026

## Introduction
Finance Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We specifically address our use of SMS and notification scraping features required for the auto-capture of financial transactions.

## Information We Collect

### 1. SMS Data
To provide automated transaction tracking, our App requests permission to read your SMS messages (`READ_SMS`).
*   **What we collect:** We only parse SMS messages from known financial institutions (banks, credit cards, digital wallets) to extract transaction amounts, merchant names, dates, and account identifiers.
*   **What we DO NOT collect:** We do not read, collect, or transmit personal SMS messages, OTPs, or messages from non-financial contacts.

### 2. Notification Data
Our App requests Notification Access (`BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE`) to auto-capture transactions from payment apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm.
*   **What we collect:** We only process notifications from recognized payment applications to extract transaction details.
*   **What we DO NOT collect:** We do not store or transmit notifications from messaging apps, social media, or any non-financial applications.

### 3. Personal Data
When you create an account, we may collect your name, email address, and authentication credentials.

## How We Use Your Information

The data we collect is used **exclusively** for the core functionality of the App:
*   To automatically log your financial transactions without manual entry.
*   To categorize your expenses and provide financial analytics (daily, monthly, yearly).
*   To synchronize your financial data securely across your devices.

## Data Security and Storage

We prioritize the security of your financial data:
*   **Local First:** Transaction parsing from SMS and notifications happens entirely locally on your device. We do not send raw SMS or notification data to our servers.
*   **Encryption:** Only parsed, structured transaction data (amount, merchant, date) is securely transmitted to our backend using HTTPS and encrypted at rest.
*   **No Data Selling:** We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

## User Control and Permissions

You have full control over the permissions granted to the App:
*   You can enable or disable **SMS Auto-Capture** and **UPI Notification Capture** at any time from the App's Profile settings or your device's operating system settings.
*   The App will continue to function (allowing manual transaction entry) even if you deny these permissions.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at support@financetracker.com.
